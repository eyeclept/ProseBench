## Rule 5: No intensifiers

"Significantly", "dramatically", "extremely" and their kin are placeholders for evidence. Replace the word with the number it was standing in for.

- WRONG: "The pricing was significantly higher than the cost of the part."
- RIGHT: "They charged $1,200 for a repair that needed a $5 chip."

The same padding shows up as a finality interjection ("full stop," "period," "end of story," "make no mistake," "plain and simple") tacked onto a claim that already carries its own exclusivity marker ("only," "never," "always," "exactly," "must"). The marker already did the emphatic work; the interjection repeats it without adding evidence.

- WRONG: "This is the only supported configuration, full stop."
- RIGHT: "This is the only supported configuration."
